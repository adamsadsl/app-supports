Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a new game?

From the home screen, tap Next puzzle, or choose a difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard, Master, or Grandmaster) and start a puzzle. You can also try the Daily Puzzle each day.

How do I use hints?

Tap Coach during a game. It highlights cells and walks you through the next step using Sudoku solving techniques.

What happens when I run out of lives?

In Ranked mode you have 3 lives, and an incorrect entry costs one. When all lives are lost, you can revive once per puzzle by watching an ad, continue the puzzle in True Zen, retry it, or start a new game. True Zen has unlimited lives.

How do I remove ads?

Buy the one-time Remove Ads purchase on the Support screen. It removes interstitial ads, makes the coach and undo unlimited, and lets you revive without watching an ad. Optional tips support the developer and do not change ads or features. If you tipped in version 1.0, your ad-free status carries over. Remove Ads can be restored from the Support screen.

Is my progress saved?

Yes! Your game progress is automatically saved when you exit. You can continue where you left off by tapping "Continue Last Puzzle" on the home screen.

How do I switch between light and dark mode?

Open Settings and choose a theme under Theme.

Do my records sync between devices?

On iOS, if you are signed in to iCloud, your play records sync through your own iCloud account. To delete them from iCloud, go to Settings > Clear iCloud Records. This also turns off sync on that device and keeps the records on the device.
