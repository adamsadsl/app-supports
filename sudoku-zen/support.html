Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a new game?

From the home screen, select a difficulty level (Easy, Medium, Hard, or Expert) to start a new puzzle. You can also try the Daily Challenge for a unique puzzle each day.

How do I use hints?

Tap the lightbulb icon during a game to get a hint. The hint will highlight cells and provide strategic suggestions based on Sudoku solving techniques.

What happens when I run out of lives?

You start each game with 3 lives. Making an incorrect entry costs one life. When all lives are lost, you can watch an ad to revive, retry the same puzzle, or start a new game.

How do I remove ads?

You can support the developer through our Tip Jar feature. Any tip will remove ads from the app permanently as a thank you for your support.

Is my progress saved?

Yes! Your game progress is automatically saved when you exit. You can continue where you left off by tapping "Continue" on the home screen.

How do I switch between light and dark mode?

Go to Settings (gear icon) and toggle the theme option. You can also set it to follow your system settings.
