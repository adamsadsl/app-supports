Privacy Policy

Sudoku Zen

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Sudoku Zen. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Sudoku Zen is designed with your privacy in mind. We collect minimal data:

  • Game Progress and Statistics: Stored on your device, and synced through your own iCloud account as described below
  • Settings & Preferences: Stored on your device and not synced
  • Advertising data: Collected by Google AdMob when ads are requested or shown (see Advertising below)

iCloud Sync and Backups

On iOS, if you are signed in to iCloud, the app saves a copy of your play records (such as statistics, completed puzzle dates, seals, technique and level progress, recent games, and a randomly generated device code) in your own iCloud key-value storage so your devices can share them. Settings and the game in progress are not synced. We run no server and cannot read this data.

You can delete this copy at any time under Settings > Clear iCloud Records. Doing so also turns off sync on that device and keeps the records on the device. Other devices that still have sync on may write their records again, so clear them there too. Restoring a device backup made before you cleared them can turn sync back on and upload the restored records. Deleting the app removes its local data but does not delete the iCloud copy.

Device backups, such as iCloud Backup or the system backup on your device, may also include the app's saved data. These backups are managed under your own account with that service.

Advertising

Sudoku Zen shows interstitial and rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To serve, personalize and measure ads, Google may collect and use the following data from your device: device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, when you allow it), advertising data, product interaction, approximate location derived from your IP address, and crash and performance diagnostics. The app has no analytics service of its own and no server that receives this data. Google may use advertising data to track users across apps and websites owned by other companies for advertising purposes, which is why we declare tracking in our App Store privacy answers.

Consent and choices. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the app shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before ads are requested; in those regions the form can be reopened under Settings > Ad Privacy Options, and in other regions this entry is not shown. On iOS, the app then asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt before the advertising identifier can be used. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized using your advertising identifier, and you can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. The consent form and the ATT prompt are shown when the app launches. If you have purchased Remove Ads, ads are not requested and these prompts are not shown.

Rewarded video ads are optional: you can watch one to receive extra undo, coach or revive credits, or to patch a missed day in your streak.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, visit: Google Privacy Policy

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We do not collect or store your payment information. The app offers an optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase (product ID remove_ads), which stops interstitial ads, makes the coach and undo unlimited and lets you revive without watching an ad, and which can be restored from the Support screen, plus optional tips that unlock no features. Whether you have purchased Remove Ads is stored locally on your device and confirmed with the store.

Data Security

The app has no server of its own and does not send your game data to us. Game data stays on your device and in your own iCloud or backup accounts, as described above.

Children's Privacy

Sudoku Zen is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
