Privacy Policy

Sudoku Zen

Last Updated: November 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Sudoku Zen. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Sudoku Zen is designed with your privacy in mind. We collect minimal data:

  • Game Progress: Stored locally on your device only
  • Settings & Preferences: Stored locally on your device only
  • Advertising data: Collected by Google AdMob only when ads are shown (see Advertising below)

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display interstitial and rewarded video ads. To serve and measure ads, AdMob (Google) may collect and use the following data from your device: device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, when you allow it), advertising data, product interaction, approximate location derived from your IP address, and crash and performance diagnostics. This data is used for third-party advertising, ad measurement and analytics. We do not receive this data and do not use it for any other purpose.

Sudoku Zen itself does not track you and does not link your data with data from other companies' apps or websites. Any advertising-related data collection described above is performed by Google's advertising SDK.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the app shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. In those regions the form can be reopened under Settings > Ad Privacy Options; in other regions this entry is not shown. On iOS, the app asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising identifier can be used for ads; if you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized using your advertising identifier. You can also limit ad tracking in your device settings.

Rewarded video ads are optional: you can watch one to receive extra undo, coach or revive credits.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, visit: Google Privacy Policy

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We do not collect or store your payment information. The app offers an optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase, which can be restored from the Support screen, and optional tips that unlock no features. Whether you have purchased Remove Ads is stored locally on your device.

Data Security

All game data is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit your personal game data to external servers.

Children's Privacy

Sudoku Zen does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is suitable for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
