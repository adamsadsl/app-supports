Privacy Policy

Sudoku Zen

Last Updated: November 4, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Sudoku Zen. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Sudoku Zen is designed with your privacy in mind. We collect minimal data:

  • Game Progress: Stored locally on your device only
  • Settings & Preferences: Stored locally on your device only
  • Advertising data: Collected by Google AdMob only when ads are shown (see Advertising below)

Advertising

Sudoku Zen shows interstitial and rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To serve, personalize and measure ads, Google may collect and use the following data from your device: device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, when you allow it), advertising data, product interaction, approximate location derived from your IP address, and crash and performance diagnostics. The app has no analytics service of its own and no server that receives this data. Google may use advertising data to track users across apps and websites owned by other companies for advertising purposes, which is why we declare tracking in our App Store privacy answers.

Consent and choices. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the app shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before ads are requested; in those regions the form can be reopened under Settings > Ad Privacy Options, and in other regions this entry is not shown. On iOS, the app then asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt before the advertising identifier can be used. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized using your advertising identifier, and you can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On a fresh install the consent form and the ATT prompt are shown only after your first completed puzzle or the first time an ad is needed, not at launch. If you have purchased Remove Ads, ads are not requested and these prompts are not shown.

Rewarded video ads are optional: you can watch one to receive extra undo, coach or revive credits.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, visit: Google Privacy Policy

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We do not collect or store your payment information. The app offers an optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase (product ID remove_ads), which stops interstitial ads and makes the undo, coach and revive credits free, and which can be restored from the Support screen, plus optional tips that unlock no features. Whether you have purchased Remove Ads is stored locally on your device and confirmed with the store.

Data Security

All game data is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit your personal game data to external servers.

Children's Privacy

Sudoku Zen does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is suitable for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
